Onna is a small brand making garments and objects with intention. Fewer things, made properly, to be worn, used and kept.

Onna began with a plain wish: clothes and objects that hold up, look right for years, and don't need to announce themselves. From across the room they read as simple. Up close, there's the stitch, the weight, and the small decisions someone made on purpose.

The name comes from Yayoi Kusama's 1953 artwork Onna (The Woman). We took it as a reminder that something honest can be quiet and still a little strange.

Our references sit outside fashion as much as in it: art, nature, cars and motorcycles, photography, workwear, vintage clothing, outdoor gear, and everyday objects that turn out to be well made.

What we hold to

Quality
Materials and making we would be happy to wear ourselves.
Simplicity
Fewer parts and clearer shapes, with nothing added for show.
Individuality
Made for people who dress for themselves, not for a look.
Craftsmanship
Small details that reward a closer look.
Sustainability
Small runs, made to last, and made once.
Honesty
We say what something is and how it is made.
